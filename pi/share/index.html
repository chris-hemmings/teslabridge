Power warning: the Pi's USB-C port becomes the music input, so the music device would also be powering the Pi through it. That may not be enough: expect drop-outs or restarts, especially during calls. For reliable use, power the Pi through the 5 V GPIO pins (pin 2/4 + ground) or a USB-C power/data splitter. The music device plugs into the Pi's USB-C port.

Home Wi-Fi

    
Networks the Pi joins by itself when they're in range (then the page is at http://auxlink.local). While the setup Wi-Fi is on, new networks are saved and joined once setup Wi-Fi switches off.

    

    

      
      
    

    

  


  
Development

    
A terminal on the Pi, the same as SSH: it logs in as the Pi user (sudo asks for that password). Turn it on in the Settings tab; it needs your own Pi login password, and a page password is recommended. It closes after 15 minutes idle.

    

    

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    

    

  


  
XIAO firmware

    
Update the XIAO on its own: unplug its USB-C from the music device, hold its B button and plug it into a USB port on the Pi (the wires to the Pi can stay). Then flash the firmware file from the releases page, or the one that came with this AuxLink. Afterwards plug it back into the music device (Android asks once: tick Always).
